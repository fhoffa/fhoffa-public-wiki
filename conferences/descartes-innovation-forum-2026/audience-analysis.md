# Audience analysis: Geotab AI Insights & MCP session

**Session:** [Fleet] Connecting AI to Action: Exploring Geotab AI Insights and MCP — Wed Oct 7, 2026, 4:30–5:30 PM CT, United A

> This is an inference from the published agenda, not a confirmed attendee list. Validate titles and headcount with Adam and the Geotab field team closer to the event.

## Who's likely in the room

- **Roles:** Fleet operations leaders, fleet managers, routing & dispatch leads, safety/compliance managers, maintenance managers, IT/integration owners, ops analysts.
- **Seniority:** Mixed. Wednesday is the training & roadmap day (87 sessions), so expect a practitioner-heavy crowd — people who live in the tools — alongside directors evaluating roadmap bets.
- **Companies:** Mid-market to enterprise shippers, carriers, and 3PLs running Descartes fleet solutions (routing, Descartes Mobile, telematics). Mostly Descartes customers and prospects; a meaningful subset will be joint Descartes + Geotab accounts.
- **Context:** They chose a Fleet-track session at 4:30 PM — they self-selected for fleet data/AI content over whatever else runs in that slot.

## Why they're there (jobs to be done)

1. **The fragmentation pain is real.** They open four apps to answer one operational question. The session premise should feel like their Tuesday morning.
2. **AI curiosity is high, AI literacy is varied.** Descartes is publicly pushing AI agents across the portfolio (see the earnings call), so the audience arrives primed — but assume most have never heard of MCP. Start from zero, no protocol jargon.
3. **They want something to take home.** Practitioners leave with the pilot blueprint; leaders leave with the governance story. Both need to survive contact with their IT/security teams.

## Energy context

- **Last slot on Wednesday, after a full training day.** Fatigue is the default state. This is why the talk plan calls for interaction roughly every 10 minutes and a demo-heavy middle.
- **United A is a breakout room, not a keynote hall.** Expect tens of people, not hundreds — conversational tone works; a broadcast tone will feel off.

## What this implies for the talk

- **Open with their pain, not with Geotab.** The "7:14 AM" story earns the next 55 minutes.
- **Demo over slides.** Ops people trust what they see. Show source attribution for every material claim — this crowd verifies.
- **Address governance head-on.** There are IT/integration owners in the room; permissions, data freshness, and failure modes are buying criteria, not footnotes.
- **Don't oversell the happy path.** One imperfect case study beats five perfect ones for credibility.
- **Finish with the pilot blueprint.** Give the practitioners a Monday-morning action and the leaders a vendor question to ask.
