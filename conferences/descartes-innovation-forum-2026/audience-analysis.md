**Published session description (verbatim):**

This session explores how Geotab AI can help connect existing fleet technology and data resources to create a more unified source of truth. See how a common set of protocols can give artificial intelligence (AI) tools consistent access to relevant information across the fleet technology stack. The session will demonstrate how combining data from telematics, safety, maintenance, operations, and other business software can help teams reduce information silos, ask better questions, and turn insights into action. Learn how Geotab AI can help organizations work from a connected operational view while preserving the governance and context needed to make confident decisions.

## What this implies for the talk

- **Open with their pain, not with Geotab.** The "7:14 AM" story earns the next 55 minutes.
- **Differentiate from the René pitch next door.** Descartes Fleet Data Intelligence runs in the same slot: one vendor's AI agent on one vendor's data. Your story is the open protocol that connects the *whole* stack — telematics, safety, maintenance, ops software — without ripping anything out. Say that contrast out loud; the audience chose between the two rooms and deserves to know what they bought.
- **Demo over slides.** Ops people trust what they see. Show source attribution for every material claim — this crowd verifies.
- **Address governance head-on.** There are IT/integration owners in the room; permissions, data freshness, and failure modes are buying criteria, not footnotes.
- **Don't oversell the happy path.** One imperfect case study beats five perfect ones for credibility.
- **Finish with the pilot blueprint.** Give the practitioners a Monday-morning action and the leaders a vendor question to ask.
